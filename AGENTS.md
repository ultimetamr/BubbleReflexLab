# BubbleReflexLab agent guide

- User-visible game name is `泡泡反应室`; the internal project, package, and Stage identifiers remain unchanged. The flat Android icon remains `ic_spatial_launcher`; PICO Home uses a two-layer spatial icon (`icon_3d_layer_0/1`) with matching SDF layers declared through `icon.3d.list` and `icon.sdf.list`. The crisp icon source is `artwork/bubble_reflex_app_icon_crisp.png`: three separated blue/gold/gray spheres on a clean navy background, with no outer bubble or broad glow.
- Android/Kotlin PICO Spatial SDK Stage app; package `com.pico.swan.bubblereflex`, launch activity `.platform.LaunchActivity`.
- Build with Java 17: `./gradlew.bat testDebugUnitTest assembleDebug`.
- Debug APK: `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk`.
- Primary gameplay rules and timing: `app/src/main/java/com/pico/swan/bubblereflex/domain/BubbleGame.kt`.
- Runtime pooled ECS sphere meshes and materials: `app/src/main/java/com/pico/swan/bubblereflex/content/BubbleSceneRenderer.kt`.
- SpatialUI/input lifecycle: `app/src/main/java/com/pico/swan/bubblereflex/content/HomeStage.kt`.
- Emulator target used for validation: `emulator-5554`; stop `com.pico.swan.palmensemble` and `com.pico.swan.tidebeacon` before visual capture because they can take Stage focus.
- Capture extras: `captureMode=tutorial`, `captureMode=gameplay`, `captureMode=feedback`, `captureMode=acceptance`.
- Current opening: sequential three-rule introduction followed by calm recap waves; nine bubbles in the first 30 seconds, constant `0.24 m/s` drift. Gold is a single opaque yellow-gold PBR sphere with no outer membrane.
- Runtime has five fixed play tiers and three 30-second cycling themes. The expanded start/tutorial/result panel is placed once from the launch camera at `(0, -0.05, -1.05)m` using `TrackingMode.ONCE`; starting play moves its child panel to a small lower-right pose. Both poses remain world-fixed after the initial sample and do not follow head motion.
- START shows no bubble entities. The normal flow has one Start click: it samples calibration and begins PLAYING immediately, while the first 30 seconds provide the in-run tutorial prompts. Playing/paused states expose end-run, and RESULT offers replay, return-to-start, and a real Activity exit.
- Music is a bright gated pentatonic pluck phrase with rests and three theme keys. Tiers 0–5 raise tempo gently from 88 to 108 BPM and add short consonant chord/sparkle layers; there is no sustained low pad/drone. Inactive/result phases pause and flush the AudioTrack; bubble drift remains `0.24 m/s`.
- Scoring is category-specific: ordinary correct `+100` / wrong `-50`, gold correct `+200` / wrong `-100`, gray untouched `+120` / touched `-120`. Contact immediately recycles the bubble and uses a six-slot pooled five-particle burst; the latest 0.9-second result is rendered above the contact point as colored `+/-` score text. Passage feedback shows points but does not falsely play a contact burst.
- Gameplay processes left/right hand pinch and slap state independently and listens to both controllers. Either tracked side keeps tracking alive; domain single-verdict adjudication prevents duplicate scoring when both sides reach one bubble. A hand grab uses the thumb/index midpoint and gets a forgiving `0.30m` gold capture radius.
- Ordinary contact has a `0.14s` grace window; gold contact has `0.75s` so a real hand can finish closing before verdict. Gold GRAB is retried throughout a held pinch and uses a relaxed `65mm` start / `85mm` latch threshold; its capture radius remains the original `0.30m`, while non-gold bubbles never inherit that assistance radius. An uncompleted touch still pops and deducts after the window; gray contact fails immediately. Controller trigger or grip retries its ray every `45ms` after a miss, consumes the hold after one successful verdict, and rearms on release.
- Preserve `design/` and `design-v2/` as invalid audit evidence. `design-v3/` is the accepted design source.
